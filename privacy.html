Easy FSC E3 Privacy Policy

Version 1.0.6 - Terms & privacy consent + 1-minute generation limit. Created by https://t.me/imkadi.

What the bot collects

By using the Telegram bot you provide your Telegram user ID and the 7-character VIN that you send yourself. The bot uses this information only to generate and deliver the requested FSC files.

Consent record

When you accept the Terms & Conditions and this Privacy Policy, the bot stores your Telegram user ID, the time of acceptance, and the terms version you accepted. This record exists so the bot knows you have agreed before generating files.

Generation logs

If statistics are enabled, a log entry (VIN, generation mode, file count, sent filename, and your Telegram user ID) may be stored to measure anonymous usage totals. VINs you generate are used only for this purpose and are not shared.

What the bot does not collect

The bot does not collect your name, phone number, location, address, or financial information. It does not track you across other applications or websites.

Data storage

Data is stored in a secured database (Supabase) behind the Telegram bot on Vercel. Access is limited to the bot and, for statistics and moderation, the bot admin (https://t.me/imkadi).

Data retention

Consent records and generation logs are kept for as long as the service operates. They are not shared, sold, or used for advertising.

Your rights

You may stop using the bot at any time. To request deletion of your stored data, contact https://t.me/imkadi.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, users may be asked to accept the updated terms and privacy policy again before continuing to use the bot.
